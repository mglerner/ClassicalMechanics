Four-spring oscillator

Taylor Fig. 5.7(b) built for real: a mass between four springs. Set the four spring constants and the start, then let it run. The path it traces is the point (x, y).

Springs (N/m)

Anchors sit 1 m from the centre. Natural length 1 m means no pretension; shorter means the springs are stretched at rest.

Start (m, m/s); mass 1 kg

Changing anything resets the motion to the new start. The title shows the small-oscillation frequencies about the equilibrium and the phase difference delta = delta_y - delta_x set by the start; the dot moves under the full spring forces, so large swings can wander from the small-oscillation picture.
